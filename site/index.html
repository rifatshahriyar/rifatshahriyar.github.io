I am a Professor at the Department of Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) of Bangladesh University of Engineering & Technology (BUET). I completed my Ph.D. in April 2015 from the Research School of Computer Science at Australian National University (ANU), where I was a member of the Computer Systems Group.

My supervisors were Steve Blackburn and Kathryn McKinley. I completed my B.Sc. in May 2007 and M.Sc. in December 2009 from CSE, BUET.
